# QA 2026-11-17: plane catch timing and the missed screens (Sky vs Space Collector)

Sky at :8766 (PERF_SKY=1), Space Collector baseline at :8767. WebKit via Playwright, headless (no GPU, so raster and compositing are not measured), Mac in normal use. Scripts: `scripts/perf/catch-plane.mjs`, `qa-first.mjs`, `qa-screens2.mjs`, `qa-sheet.mjs`. Output: `scripts/perf/out/catch/` (logs, shots) and `scripts/perf/out/qa/` (shots), side-by-side sheets in `scripts/perf/out/qa/sheets/` (space left, sky right, no labels). App code was not touched.

## Task L: catch to reveal

2,000 seeded cards, 390x844. Sky: day 13:10, Show me opens Today, then the view is steered straight onto each plane (`window.__sky.drag`) until the Collect ticket shows a new card. Space: evening 19:40, `__aim()` onto a satellite, as budget.mjs. Limits (budget.mjs): worst frame 50 ms, at most 2 frames over 33 ms, judged on the median of 3.

| Flow | App | Run 1 max / >33 / >50 | Run 2 | Run 3 | Median max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collect -> reveal | Sky | 64 / 1 / 1 | 45 / 1 / 0 | 54 / 1 / 1 | 54 (over 50) |
| Collect -> reveal | Space | 58 / 1 / 1 | 40 / 2 / 0 | 34 / 0 / 0 | 40 |
| Reveal close | Sky | 88 / 1 / 1 | 32 / 0 / 0 | 32 / 0 / 0 | 32 |
| Reveal close | Space | 57 / 2 / 1 | 39 / 1 / 0 | 23 / 0 / 0 | 39 |

(p50 17 ms and p95 18 ms in every run, both apps.)

What the harness says was in the long frames:
- Sky collect (64, 45, 54 ms): always the first frame after the click, "no callback (style/layout/paint/GC/decode)". The click handlers themselves are 8 ms (main.js:1469 and 1499). Reveal rAFs (reveal.js:59, :425) are 1 ms max. So it is style/layout/paint of the reveal opening, not script.
- Sky close, run 1 (88 ms, 242 ms in): `raf main.js:7852` (the sky loop) at 18 ms plus the close click handler (main.js:4246, 10 ms) and, in the Collection frame, flight-card.js:99 (13 ms) and cards-page.js:289 (5 ms). Runs 2 and 3 were clean, so this looks like a one-off cold frame.
- Space collect (58 ms, run 1): same as Sky, first frame, no callback. Space close run 1 had a 57 ms frame with no callback and a 43 ms frame at 1494 ms (`raf card.js:221` 10 ms, plus harness 26 ms).
- Verdict: Sky and Space behave the same here. Both have a one-off 45-65 ms first frame on Collect. Sky's median (54) is just over the 50 ms limit and Space's (40) just under, but with three runs on a busy Mac that difference is within noise. I would not call it a Sky regression. Not run: Chromium 4x, an iPhone.
- Note: main.js line numbers moved by 3 between my first and later runs (7849 vs 7852), so someone edited main.js during the session.

## Task M: findings (most important first)

Sheets referenced below are in `scripts/perf/out/qa/sheets/`.

1. **Card back picker is taller than the screen and cannot be scrolled (Sky).** The sheet is 1,139 px tall at 390x844, with its top at -295 px, so the handle, the "Card back" title, the intro line and the first row of backs are off-screen. Nothing inside it scrolls (probed: scrollHeight equals clientHeight). Space's sheet is 460 px and fits. Shots: `out/qa/sky-390x844-backs.png`, `sky-320x640-backs.png`, sheet `backs-390.png`, `m320-c.png`. Sure (measured). It is worse at 320x640 (not measured numerically, same layout).
2. **Welcome card is clipped at 320x640 (Sky).** The "SWIPE UP TO BEGIN / SKY COLLECTOR" footer is cut off at the bottom of the card, because the Sky copy is longer than Space's (7 lines of body text vs 5). Shot: `sky-320x640-first-2-welcome.png`, sheet `m320-a.png`. Sure.
3. **Orange still in Sky.** Red is the rule, but these are orange:
   - the how-to art on the welcome card (circle and dashed route in picture 2, route in 1 and 3) and in the tour's "Catch a plane" card (`sky-390x844-first-3-tour3.png`);
   - How to play slide art (the circle, "Show me" pill, bell);
   - the Collection rank card: route line, dots and "NEXT" label (`sky-390x844-day-05-viewer.png` from the earlier pass, and `viewer-390.png`);
   - the Explore "turn this way" arrow button in a circle (`out/catch/sky-run1-aimed.png`, `sky-390x844-aimed.png`) and the plane glyph in the circle during a catch (that one looks red-orange, `#ff6a4a`-ish, probably intended);
   - the flight card's route dashes, plane dots and the arrow after the origin city (`out/qa/sheets/z-reveal-sky.png`);
   - the Settings sliders and toggles in Space are orange and Sky has red (fine).
   Fairly sure it is a leftover, but I can't tell which of these are meant to be the "card orange" accent (the flight card's dashes and arrow may be a deliberate colour).
4. **How to play slides 3, 4, 5 and 6 (Sky): a lighter blue square sits behind the icon** (visible in `howto-04..06` and `howto-b.png`, `m320-d.png`). It looks like a leftover tile from a dark-theme icon, not a full-width picture like slides 1 and 2. On slide 6 the "Show me" text is wider than its orange pill (overflows both sides, also at 390). Fairly sure.
5. **Reveal header chips clip at 320 (Sky).** "Sky challenge +50" is cut off at the right edge (chip row doesn't wrap or scroll). Shot: `sky-320x640-reveal-done.png`, `m320-c.png`. Sure. At 390 both chips fit.
6. **Sheet grab handle is a wide pale-blue block, not a thin bar (Sky),** on the How to play sheet (Space: thin grey bar). Shot: `sky-390x844-howto-01.png`. Sure it differs; would call it low priority.
7. **Dark panels in a light Sky.** The Collect ticket on Explore (`sky-390x844-aimed.png`) and the "To your left, drag" guide ticket (`out/catch/sky-run1-aimed.png`) are near-black cards on a daylight sky; the tour's SKIP pill is dark too. The Settings page behind the sheets looks light in Sky. If the intent is light everywhere except the Explore header at night, the ticket is the main outlier. Not sure it is wrong (Space uses the same dark ticket).
8. **Leftover star content in Explore (Sky).** A "HYDRA" constellation label and faint constellation lines show under the horizon in daytime (`sky-390x844-aimed.png`). Sure it is leftover; low impact.
9. **Collection tabs differ.** Space: Mine / Albums / Patches / Find; Sky: Mine / Patches only (`viewer-390.png` earlier sheet, `sky-390x844-day-04-collection.png`). Probably intended (no catalogue to browse yet), not sure.
10. **Card photo letterboxing (Sky).** A portrait-shaped plane photo (UAL582 reveal) sits in a wider frame with a slightly different blue strip on each side (`z-reveal-sky.png`). Cosmetic; the Cessna type card uses a crude "T" silhouette with a faint "C25B" watermark (`sky-390x844-viewer-front.png`). Sure it is visible; plane content, not code.
11. **Tour differs in length.** Sky has 2 steps (location, motion) plus the catch card; Space has 3 plus the catch card (notifications is the third). Sky also shows "24 OVERHEAD" on the welcome card and compass. Presumably intended. Space has an extra header icon (clock/peak toggle) that Sky drops. Presumably intended.
12. **No leftover "Space Collector" text found in Sky** on the screens captured (Settings rows read "Sky Collector Plus"; the reveal footer says "Sky Collector"). Not exhaustive.
13. **Card back: other differences are fine.** The viewer front and flipped back (`viewer-390.png`) match in layout; the back is the new Sky art; the viewer lacks "Tap for its orbit" and the "Now over the North Pacific" line (satellite-only, intended).

Checked and fine at both sizes: sealed pack and tear (`first-390-a.png`), tour steps 1-2 layout and buttons, card viewer front/back layout, reveal mid and finished card with its buttons at 390.
