# Photo coverage and framing pass, 2026-11-17

Pipeline additions (all in scripts/photos/): `make-gap-jobs.py` (writes gap-jobs.json), `find-gap-photos.py` (Commons search, licence/size
filter, box detection, cover-at-290x200 check, contact sheets), `merge-gap-picks.py` (hand picks in gap-picks.json go into the existing
house-/airline- candidates and picks files), `set-boxes.py` (hand-drawn tight boxes into box-overrides.json). The existing
apply-house-photos.py / apply-airline-photos.py / make-sizes.py then did the downloading, module writing and sm/tiny copies.
Every added photo was looked at on a contact sheet with a 10% grid, and a tight box (nose to tail, wingtip to wingtip) was drawn by eye.

## Framing (task J)

Done for the 40 most common 'contain' airline photos: 20 replaced. The rest were left because no candidate that fits 'cover' at 290x200 was
clearly better (head-on / underside / ground-clutter only, or the detector found none). About 209 airline photos and all 75 type photos
(types/) are still 'contain'; most are wide side-on shots where the plane spans the whole photo, so a card crop would cut it.
Remaining 'contain' list can be regenerated with the audit in this doc's method (fitStyle at 290x200 and 460x200).
