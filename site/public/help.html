Something’s wrong

Planes on screen aren’t where the real ones are

Your phone’s compass is almost always the cause. Line it up against something you can see:

  1. Open Settings → Aiming → Compass and tap Line up.
  2. Point the circle at the thing it names: the Sun by day, or the Moon, a planet or a bright star at night. Aim the phone at the Sun, never your eyes.
  3. If there’s nothing to aim at, it asks you to point at north, or to drag the sky until it matches.

To undo it, tap Reset in the same place.

  • “Compass · Needs a nudge”: wave the phone in a slow figure-8, away from metal.
  • “Sky drifting? Line it up with something you can see”: tap it. It opens the same steps.
  • “Approximate · planes may be drawn a little off”: your phone is sharing only a rough location. See the next but one answer.
The sky doesn’t move when I turn my phone
  1. If the circle says Tap to turn on motion or Tap to wake the sky, tap it. Motion pauses whenever you leave the app.
  2. If that doesn’t work, open Settings → Your sky → Look around with, and choose Motion.
  3. If it says “Motion is unavailable”, choose Finger there and drag the sky to look around.
The app has the wrong place, or can’t find me

Open Settings → Your sky → Location.

  1. Tap Use my location and allow it when your phone asks.
  2. Or search for a city or town, or type coordinates.
  • “Location is off for Sky Collector”: turn it on in your phone’s Settings → Sky Collector → Location, or search for a place.
  • “Location is switched off on this phone”: turn on location in your phone’s settings.
  • “Couldn’t find you just now”: try again outside, or search for a place.
  • If the sky is a city “picked from your phone’s time zone”, the app doesn’t have your place yet.

Planes are close and fast, so a precise location draws them in the right spot. With a rough one they may be a little off.

There are no planes
  • “Quiet sky”: nothing is overhead right now. Today lists what’s coming over in the next 15 minutes.
  • “Can’t reach the plane feed” or “Plane feed down · retrying”: the app can’t get plane positions. Check your connection. It keeps trying.
  • “No plane tracking here yet”: plane positions come from volunteers’ receivers, and none reach your area yet.
  • “None up high enough yet”: planes are near, but too low on your horizon to catch.
  • Check Settings → What shows → Planes is on.
I can see a plane and the app doesn’t show it

Not every aircraft broadcasts its position, and some are out of reach of the receivers the app relies on. If the app shows it in the wrong place instead, line up the compass (the first answer on this page).

It won’t let me catch a plane
  • “Too low to catch”: wait for it to climb higher above your horizon.
  • “Too far to catch”: it has to come closer. Small aircraft need to be nearer than big ones, and the app shows how far away it is.
  • “Caught today”: you’ve already caught this aircraft today. Tap View card to see it.
A plane has no airline or route

Only airline flights show an airline and a route. Private, business, military and government aircraft show what kind of aircraft they are, and nothing else. That’s on purpose.

Sometimes an airline flight’s route isn’t known either. Its card then says “route not known”, and a later catch of the same flight can fill it in.

There’s no sound, or no music
  1. Open Settings → Sound and check that Sounds and Music are on and their volume sliders are up.
  2. Check the silent switch on the side of the phone. Sky Collector stays quiet when it is on.
  3. Turn up your phone’s volume. Music starts after your first tap.
A plane alert didn’t arrive
  1. If Settings → Notifications says “Notifications are off on this phone”, turn them on in your phone’s Settings → Sky Collector.
  2. Check Plane alerts is on, and check your quiet hours, in the same place.
  3. Alerts are set as you leave the app, from planes already on their way. If you haven’t opened the app for a while, there’s nothing to alert you about.
  4. There are three a day at most, at least half an hour apart.
My purchase didn’t go through, or Plus isn’t on
  1. Open Settings → Collection → Sky Collector Plus.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • “The purchase didn’t go through”: you haven’t been charged. Try again in a moment.
  • “Waiting for approval”: a family organiser has to approve it. Plus turns on as soon as they do.
  • “No Plus purchase on this Apple Account”: you’re signed in to a different account from the one that bought it.
  • “Couldn’t reach the App Store”: check your connection and try again.
A pack someone sent me won’t open
  • “These cards have expired”: packs last 90 days. Ask for them to be shared again.
  • “No connection”: tap the link again once you’re online.
  • “Update to open these”: update Sky Collector, then tap the link again.
  • “The link was cut short”: ask them to send it again.
  • “Already in your collection” or “Already shared”: you have every card in it already.
The app says “Couldn’t save”

Your phone’s storage is full or blocked. Free up some space, then catch it again. In a web browser, close private browsing. Nothing already in your collection is affected.

The text is too small, or the app won’t turn

The app follows your phone’s text size setting, up to a point. On phones it is upright only. Tablets can turn.

Getting started

How do I play?

Point your phone at a plane. When it lines up in the circle, the app says what it is. Tap Collect and its card is yours. If it’s an airline flight, you get a second card too: a boarding pass for that flight.

Do I have to be outside?

No. The app draws planes where they really are, even through a ceiling. If you’d rather not wave your phone about, choose Finger in Settings → Your sky → Look around with, and drag the sky.

What can I collect?

Two kinds of card. Plane cards are for the kind of aircraft: an A320, a 747, a helicopter. Boarding passes are for airline flights: the airline, the flight number and where it’s going. One catch can give you both.

What is the Today page?

What’s flying over you today: what’s up now, what’s coming over in the next 15 minutes, where those planes are going, your regulars, and your day so far, with the farthest, highest and fastest you’ve caught. Tap Show me beside a plane and the app points you to it.

When does Sky Collector come out?

Soon. There’s no date yet, and it isn’t in any store today. skycollector.app will say when it is.

Is it free?

Yes. Every card, patch and mission is free to collect, and there are no ads. An optional membership, Sky Collector Plus, adds extras.

Cards

What do Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary mean?

How unusual the catch is. For plane cards, it’s how rarely that kind of aircraft is seen. For boarding passes, it’s how far the flight is going: the longest flights are Legendary. Each rarity has its own shape on the card. Rarity is never something you pay for.

What do Bronze, Silver and Gold mean?

How many times you’ve caught it. Cards level up as you catch the same kind of plane, or the same flight, on more days.

What is a shiny card?

A card from a lucky moment. There are three:

  • Moon crossing: the plane was right beside the Moon.
  • Golden hour: your first catch in the few minutes the sun sits on the horizon.
  • New colours: a rare aircraft you already have, caught flying for an airline you haven’t seen it with.
What are albums?

Cards sort into 19 albums, such as Giants, Helicopters, Long Haul and Cargo Runs. Each has bronze, silver and gold goals, and a patch.

What are regulars?

Airline flights that cross your sky at about the same time on most days. Once you’ve seen one on three of the last seven days, with the app open, it becomes a regular. Today shows when your regulars are next due.

What are story cards and secrets?

A few things are hidden. If something odd lines up in the circle, hold the circle still on it for a few seconds. That’s all we’ll say.

Today and alerts

What does the app send without being asked?

One kind of notification: Plane alerts. A new card or a rare plane about to fly over you, or one of your regulars a few minutes before its usual time. Three a day at most, and never in your quiet hours. Turn them on or off in Settings → Notifications.

What are quiet hours?

The hours when the app won’t send anything: 11 PM to 7 AM unless you change them in Settings → Notifications.

What are the widgets?

Widgets show what’s coming over next. The small one is free. The medium, large and Lock Screen widgets come with Plus.

What is the pack I get in the morning?

After a day or night of catching, your best cards from it wait as a sealed pack. Tear it open to turn them over, then share them if you like.

Patches and missions

What are missions?

Three small goals each day: one easy, one harder, one a stretch. They’re sized to your sky, so each can really be done. Each one you finish earns XP. A themed week runs from Monday as well, with its own patch.

What are patches?

Awards for milestones: your first card, a finished album, a themed week, a secret found. Tap one on the wall to see how to earn it.

What are ranks?

Everything you catch earns XP, and XP raises your rank: Skywatcher, Spotter, Type Spotter, Plane Expert, Sky Scholar, Sky Master, Sky Legend, Hall of Fame.

How do I earn the Ambassador patch?

Tell a friend. Settings → Help → Tell a friend sends them a sample pack of three cards that opens in their browser, no app needed.

Sky Collector Plus

What is Plus?

Optional extras for players who want to support the game. Everything you collect and earn is free without it. Plus adds every card back, seven more app icons, the larger widgets and a members’ patch.

What does it cost, and is there a free trial?

You can pay monthly, yearly, or once to keep it for good. The yearly plan starts with a free week for new subscribers. The store shows the price in your own currency.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple Account settings, under Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it. You keep every card, and the members’ patch stays on your wall for good.

I have a code

Open Settings → Collection → Sky Collector Plus, scroll to the bottom and tap Redeem a code.

Your collection

How do I send cards to a friend?

Open a card, tap the share icon and choose Send the card. Or go to Settings → Collection → Send cards to pick several. Your friend gets a link that opens as a pack, and you keep your own cards. The link works for 90 days. A pack never says where you were.

Do cards I’m sent count towards my rank?

They join your collection, but they don’t add XP, levels or patches. Only what you catch yourself does.

How do I move to a new phone?

Your collection backs up to your own iCloud by itself. Sign in to the same iCloud on the new phone and open the app. There are no accounts.

Can I change how my cards and app look?

Settings → Collection → Card back changes the back of every card. Two backs are free and the rest come with Plus. App icon changes the icon on your Home Screen.

Where are my numbers?

Settings → Collection → Spotter stats.

How do I start again?

Settings → About → Reset everything. It deletes your cards, patches and settings, here and in your iCloud backup, and can’t be undone.

Privacy

Does it use my location?

Yes, to work out which planes are over you. That is worked out on your phone. To get the planes and the weather near you, the app sends a rounded position, a point several kilometres across, never your exact one. It is never sold. You can pick a city in Settings instead.

What does it show about the planes?

For airline flights: the airline, the flight number and the route. For every other aircraft, private, business, military or government: only what kind of aircraft it is. No name, no registration, no route. The app keeps no history of where any aircraft has been.

What do you keep about me?

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your collection stays on your phone and in your own backup. The app sends a few anonymous notes about how it is used, such as crashes and play counts, never your location. You can turn them off in Settings → Help → Share anonymous usage. The full policy is at skycollector.app/privacy.
