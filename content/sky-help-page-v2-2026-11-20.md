## Part 1. The page

# Help

## Getting started

**How do I play?**
Point your phone at a plane. When it lines up in the circle, tap Collect. You get a card for that kind of plane, and for most airline flights a second card with the airline and where it's going.

**Do I have to be outside?**
No. The app draws the sky wherever you point, so a window works, and so does a garden or the fence by an airport. If your phone has no motion sensors, you can drag the sky with a finger.

**Nothing is overhead. What now?**
Open Today and tap Show me. It points you at the best plane up now, and lists what is due over you in the next 15 minutes.

## When something looks wrong

**I can see a plane, but the app doesn't show it.**
The app shows what volunteers with receivers pick up and share. Coverage is best in North America and Europe and thinner elsewhere, and some aircraft don't broadcast at all. So now and then a real plane has no card.

**The app shows a plane I can't see.**
It may be behind cloud, a building or trees. Planes very low on the horizon are left out for that reason. If planes are often in the wrong place, line up the compass (next answer).

**The planes on screen aren't where the real ones are.**
The compass in your phone is usually the cause. Go to Settings → Compass and tap Line up. It asks you to point the circle at something you can see and sets itself from that. By day it uses the Sun: aim the phone at it, never your eyes.

**The sky doesn't move when I turn my phone.**
Go to Settings → Look around with, and choose Motion. Allow motion if your phone asks. If your phone has no motion sensors, choose Finger and drag the sky.

**Why did I only get one card for an airliner?**
The second card needs the airline and the route, and we don't always know them. You still get the card for the kind of plane. If the same flight passes again once we know more, it can earn its flight card then.

**Why doesn't this plane show an airline or a route?**
Private and business aircraft, and military, police and government aircraft, show only what kind of aircraft they are. That is on purpose. Sky Collector is a collecting game, not a flight tracker, and it can't follow a particular plane or person.

## Cards

**What do Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary mean?**
For a plane card, how unusual that kind of plane is. For a flight card, how far the flight goes. Rarity is never something you pay for. Each has its own shape on the card.

**What do Bronze, Silver and Gold mean?**
Cards level up when you catch them again. A flight card turns silver when you've caught that flight on 5 days, and gold on 15. A plane card turns silver after 10 different aircraft of that kind, and gold after 50.

**What is a stamp?**
The mark a card gets each time you add to it: the same flight on another day, or another aircraft of the same kind.

**What is a shiny card?**
A card from a lucky moment: a plane passing right beside the Moon, a plane caught as the sun sits on the horizon, or a rare kind of plane in an airline's colours you haven't collected before.

**What are sets?**
Groups of cards to fill, like The 737 Family, Giants or Short Hops. There are nineteen. Each has a bronze, silver and gold goal, so a quiet sky can still finish one.

**What are my regulars?**
Airline flights that pass over you at about the same time most days. After a few days the app spots them and tells you when each is usually due. It works this out on your phone. Only airline flights can be regulars.

## Missions and patches

**What are missions?**
Three small goals a day, chosen to fit how busy your sky is. A new themed week starts every Monday. Each one you finish earns XP.

**What are patches and ranks?**
Patches are awards for milestones, like your first card or a finished set. XP raises your rank. All of it is free to earn.

**What is the pack I get in the morning?**
After a day of catching, your cards from that day wait as a sealed pack. Tear it open and turn over the best ones.

## Alerts

**How do plane alerts work?**
They are free, and off until you turn them on in Settings → Notifications. When you leave the app, it can set an alert for a rare plane or a new card due over you in the next few minutes, or for a regular flight you haven't caught. You get three a day at most, never in your quiet hours.

**Why didn't I get an alert for a plane that flew over?**
The app can't look for planes while it's closed. It can only warn you about ones it could already see coming when you left, and about your regulars. Alerts work best on days you've opened the app.

**What are the widgets?**
On iPhone, widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. The medium, large and Lock Screen ones come with Plus. There are no widgets on Android.

## Sky Collector Plus

**What is Plus?**
Optional extras for players who want to support the game. Everything you collect and earn is free without it, and so are plane alerts. Plus adds every card back, seven more app icons, widgets on iPhone and a members' patch.

**What does it cost, and is there a free trial?**
You can pay monthly, yearly, or once to keep it for good. The yearly plan starts with a free week for new subscribers. Your store shows the price in your own currency. Plus is on iPhone now and is coming to Android.

**I bought Plus and it isn't on (or I have a new phone).**
Open the Plus page, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I cancel?**
In your Apple Account settings, at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. You keep every card, and the members' patch stays on your wall.

## Your collection

**How do I send cards to a friend?**
Open a card, tap the share icon and choose Send the card. Your friend gets a link that opens as a pack, and you keep your own cards. The link works for 90 days. A pack never includes where you were.

**Do cards I'm sent count towards my rank?**
They join your collection, but they don't add XP, levels or patches. Only what you catch yourself does.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
On iPhone, your collection backs up to your own iCloud by itself. Sign in to the same iCloud on the new phone and open the app. On Android, it should come across with your phone's Google backup, if you have that turned on. There are no accounts, so a collection can't move between iPhone and Android.

**How do I start again?**
Settings → Reset everything. It deletes your cards, patches, settings and reminders from this phone, and your iCloud backup if you have one. It can't be undone.

## Privacy

**Does it use my location?**
Yes, to place each plane in your sky. Your exact position stays on your phone. Only a position rounded to about 5 km is sent, to find the planes near you. You can pick a city in Settings instead.

**What do you keep about me?**
There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your collection stays on your phone and in your own backup. The app sends a few anonymous notes about how it is used, with no location, and you can turn them off in Settings → Help → Share anonymous usage. The full policy is at skycollector.app/privacy.

## Contact

**How do I reach you?**
Write to hello@skycollector.app, or tap Settings → Help → Send feedback. Tell us which phone you have and what you were doing when it went wrong.
