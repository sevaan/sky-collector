Note for Boss: Sky's thresholds are not exactly Space's. Ranks 2 and 3 are 480 and 2,000 (Space: 500 and 1,500), set so the first rank-up lands on day 2. I used **Sky's numbers** below. Names do not depend on them.

Why the current names should change, against the brief:
- **Tracker** reads as tracking something (or someone). Out.
- **Air Traffic Controller** and **Chief Pilot** are real jobs, and "Chief Pilot" says you fly for someone. Out.
- **Ace** is a military fighter-pilot word. Out.
- **Navigator** is an old crew job. Fine for a ship, weak here. I dropped it from all three ladders.
- **Plane Spotter** and **Sky Legend** are fine; both are in ladder A.

## How a rank shows up (so the names fit)

- The rank card and the Observer stats show the rank name and "N XP to <next rank>" ("240 XP to Spotter"). The rank-up notice says "New rank" and the name.
- The ladder picture shows four rungs around yours.
- A name longer than 12 characters with a space wraps to two lines on the rank card (Space's "Flight Controller" ran off the card once). Every name below is **12 characters or fewer**.
- Names appear in capitals on the card; I wrote them in normal case.

## Rules I wrote to

Same count (8) and the thresholds above. Plain enough for a 10-year-old and a non-native reader. No military ranks. No job titles that imply the player flies or works for someone. Nothing that means following a person or a plane. No emergency words. Each name is short, one or two words, and each makes sense as "You are a ____".

## Ladder A: Look up and learn (my pick)

You start as someone who looks up, then you get good at telling planes apart. The words are a hobbyist's, not a job's.

| # | XP | Rank | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Skywatcher | 10 |
| 2 | 480 | Spotter | 7 |
| 3 | 2,000 | Type Spotter | 12 |
| 4 | 4,000 | Plane Expert | 12 |
| 5 | 10,000 | Sky Scholar | 11 |
| 6 | 25,000 | Sky Master | 10 |
| 7 | 50,000 | Sky Legend | 10 |
| 8 | 90,000 | Hall of Fame | 12 |

**Why it's first:** it is the same family as Space (Space starts at "Stargazer" and passes through "Spotter"; Sky starts at "Skywatcher" and keeps "Spotter"), every name is a hobby or a skill and none is a job, and "Type Spotter" teaches the thing the game is about: telling aircraft types apart.
**Watch:** "Hall of Fame" is a place, not a person ("You are Hall of Fame"). If that reads oddly, swap in "Sky Elder" or "Sky Hero".

## Ladder B: Flight stages

A whole flight, from the gate up to the stratosphere. Each rank is a stage of a flight. Playful and easy to remember.

| # | XP | Rank | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Boarding | 8 |
| 2 | 480 | Taxiing | 7 |
| 3 | 2,000 | Takeoff | 7 |
| 4 | 4,000 | Climbing | 8 |
| 5 | 10,000 | Cruising | 8 |
| 6 | 25,000 | High Flyer | 10 |
| 7 | 50,000 | Jet Stream | 10 |
| 8 | 90,000 | Stratosphere | 12 |

**Why second:** the clearest picture of "going up", and no job or military anywhere. **Weakness:** they are things a plane does, not what the player is ("Rank: Taxiing"), and it drops "Spotter", which Sky and Space share. "Jet Stream" may need a one-line explanation for a young player.

## Ladder C: Aircraft through time

A history of aircraft, from a glider to a hypersonic jet. Each name is a real kind of aircraft, so the ladder teaches.

| # | XP | Rank | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Glider | 6 |
| 2 | 480 | Biplane | 7 |
| 3 | 2,000 | Propeller | 9 |
| 4 | 4,000 | Turboprop | 9 |
| 5 | 10,000 | Jet | 3 |
| 6 | 25,000 | Jumbo | 5 |
| 7 | 50,000 | Supersonic | 10 |
| 8 | 90,000 | Hypersonic | 10 |

**Why third:** the most "about aircraft" of the three, and every rank is true. **Weakness:** "Turboprop" and "Hypersonic" are harder words for a ten-year-old and a non-native reader, "Jet" is very short for a rank, and "Jumbo" is a nickname. Not checked: whether anything is dated in a way that could go stale ("Supersonic" and "Hypersonic" are fine as general words).

## Checks done on all three

- Count: 8 names each, matching Sky's 8 thresholds.
- Length: all names 12 characters or fewer.
- Not military (no Captain, Major, Ace, Commander); not a job (no Pilot, Controller, Navigator, Steward, Flight Attendant); not tracking; no emergency words.
- None collides with a Sky patch, card back or rank-up wording I could find. Sky's own patches I saw are alliance names (Star Alliance, oneworld, SkyTeam); the full list is not in my sweep.
- Not checked: how each name looks on the rank card at 320 px wide, and in the ladder picture's four rungs. Sky Dev should look at the longest ones ("Type Spotter", "Plane Expert", "Hall of Fame", "Stratosphere").
