## 5. Description

### 5a. App Store description (3,681 of 4,000)

```
Hold your phone up to the sky and find out what's flying over you. Line a plane up in the circle, tap Collect, and it's yours: a card for the kind of plane and, for most airline flights, a second card for the flight itself.

No experience needed. Free, no ads, no account, no loot boxes.

WHAT YOU CAN COLLECT
• Airline flights: a card with the airline and the route, when we know it
• Plane cards for each kind of aircraft, from small propeller planes to jumbo jets
• Helicopters, gliders, hot-air balloons and airships, when one goes by
• Rare visitors: giant freighters and old classics
Many cards carry a real photograph, with the photographer's name on the card.

SEE WHAT'S COMING OVER
The Today page shows the best plane overhead right now, the planes about to climb into your sky, and how many you've seen so far today. Tap Show me and the app points the way. Nothing overhead? Show me finds you a plane you can catch.

YOUR REGULARS
Some flights pass you at about the same time every day. After a few days Sky Collector spots them and tells you when each one usually comes over. It works this out on your phone and never sends it anywhere.

HOW RARITY WORKS
A plane card's rarity is how unusual that kind of plane is. A flight card's rarity is how far the flight goes. Rarity is never something you pay for.

LEVEL UP
Catch the same flight on 5 different days and its card turns silver. On 15, gold. For a kind of plane it takes 10 and 50 aircraft.

SHINY CARDS
A card comes out shiny when you catch it at the right moment: a plane passing right beside the Moon, a plane as the sun goes down or comes up, or a rare kind of plane in an airline's colours you haven't collected before.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• New missions every day, and a themed week every week
• Patches for airlines, countries, long flights and more
• Catch something new and the next morning your day waits as a pack of cards to tear open

SEND CARDS TO A FRIEND
Pick cards, or whole days, and send them as a link. On your friend's phone they open as a pack. You keep your own cards. Or send a friend a sample pack that opens in any browser.

ALERTS (optional)
Turn on alerts and, when you leave the app, Sky Collector can set a heads-up for a rare plane or a new card due over you in the next few minutes, or for a regular flight you haven't caught. Three a day at most, never in your quiet hours. It can't spot new planes while the app is closed.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plane positions come from volunteers with receivers (adsb.lol, with a backup feed). Coverage is strongest in North America and Europe and thinner in other places, and not every aircraft is shown.
• Most airline flights show the airline and the route; if we don't know the route, you still get the plane card. Private, business, military, police and government aircraft show only the kind of aircraft.
• Sky Collector can't follow a particular plane or person, and it doesn't try to.

SKY COLLECTOR PLUS (optional)
Medium, large and Lock Screen widgets for your regular flights (iPhone), every card back, seven more app icons and a members-only patch. Monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) or once, for good. Shared with Family Sharing.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used to draw each plane where it really is in your sky, and your exact position stays on your phone. Only a rounded position, about 5 km, is sent to get nearby planes.
Privacy: https://skycollector.app/privacy
```

## 6. What I checked, and what I could not

**Confirmed in Sky's code (2026-11-20)**
- Collect a plane; an airline flight gives two cards: the flight (airline and where it's going) and the plane (aircraft type). `HT_SLIDES_SKY`, `js/main.js`.
- Helicopters, gliders, balloons, airships: `js/planes.js` types (`balloon`, `airship`, `glider`, `heli`), `js/flight-card.js`.
- Today page: Up now, Coming over next (planes about to climb above 15° in the next 15 minutes), Today so far; Show me. `js/sky-today.js` header; "Nothing overhead? Open Today and tap Show me".
- Regulars: airline flights that pass at about the same time each day, learned on the phone, never sent; needs at least 3 days of the same flight; private aircraft never become regulars. `js/sky-regulars.js` header.
- Rarity: plane card = how much of the world's traffic the type is (hand-lifted for icons); flight card = distance. `docs/DESIGN-LOOP.md` section 1 and `js/flights.js`. **So "rarity is how hard something is to see" is true for plane cards and not for flight cards.** I wrote it the true way (5a, HOW RARITY WORKS) rather than copy Space's line.
- Levels: flights at 5 and 15 days, plane types at 10 and 50 aircraft; Bronze, Silver, Gold. `HT_SLIDES_SKY`; `SKY_OVER.silver/gold`.
- Shiny: beside the Moon, the sun on the horizon (golden hour), new colours on a Rare-or-rarer type. `js/shiny.js shinyForPlane`.
- Missions every day; a themed week (12 themes). `SKY_MISSIONS`, `SKY_WEEK_THEMES` in `js/progress.js`.
- Plane alerts: opt-in, off until turned on, at most 3 a day, 30 minutes apart, never in quiet hours, about 2 minutes before. `js/sky-alerts.js`. **Free.** The settings text reads "A new card or a rare plane about to fly over you. At most 3 a day."
- Morning pack ("Yesterday · your day, in a pack"); Send cards as a link that opens as a pack; Tell a friend with a browser sample pack at skycollector.app/pack. Same code as Space Collector (`nightPackData`, `send-cards.js`, `TELL_URL`).
- Plus page (Sky): card backs, widgets (medium, large, Lock Screen; "iPhone only"), seven more app icons, member patch. `KIT`, `PLUS_PERKS` in `js/main.js` / `js/plus.js`. Prices as Space: $1.99 / $14.99 with a 7-day trial on the year / $29.99. Family Sharing is on for all three (`docs/STORES.md`).
- Aircraft that are not airline flights show the type only; plane data from adsb.lol with a backup relay; rounded position about 5 km; precise position stays on the phone; no ads, no accounts. `privacy.html`, `design/privacy-store-checklist.md`.

**Could not confirm, or differs from the brief**

The code says "Plane alerts, free, off until turned on" (`js/main.js` ~5937, `js/sky-alerts.js`, `js/plus.js` comment: "plane alerts are free here"). I wrote the listing the way the code works. If Plus should include them, the code changes, not just the text.
2. **No streak widget found.** The brief lists "streak" among the widgets. The code sends Space's widget layouts fed with your regulars (`sendSkyWidgets`). The Plus page says "Medium, large and Lock Screen widgets for your regulars. iPhone only." I did not claim a streak widget.
3. **The widget extension itself is not in this checkout** (no Swift sources; `docs/STORES.md` says the phone-app shell is not in the repo yet). I claimed the widgets from the JS side and the Plus page only. Whoever builds confirm they ship in the first build. The "small one is free" is from a code comment.
4. **Android widgets and Plus on Android:** none and unconfirmed. The Android text leaves widgets out.
5. **The morning pack and Send cards in Sky:** the code is shared with Space; I did not see them play in Sky.
6. **Number of patches, cards, airlines:** not claimed. I did not count.
7. **Photos:** "many cards carry a real photograph" is from the privacy page (each photo's author is on its card). I did not count them; `docs/PROJECT-PLAN.md` once said 75 types.
8. **The privacy page is still the test version.** `privacy.html` says it covers the test site and will be rewritten before the stores. It must be rewritten and live at skycollector.app/privacy before submission. This is the biggest blocker for the store forms.

## 8. Short privacy answers for the store forms (plain words)

For App Store Connect's App Privacy labels and Google Play's Data safety form. Detail and reasoning: `design/privacy-store-checklist.md`; nothing here has been entered in either console. Source for every line: that checklist and `privacy.html`.

**In one sentence:** Sky Collector uses your location to draw planes where they really are, keeps your exact position on your phone, and has no accounts, ads, tracking or selling of data.

| Question | Plain answer |
|---|---|
| Do you collect precise location? | The app asks for it and uses it only on the phone, to place each plane in your sky. It is not stored on our server. A position rounded to about 5 km is sent to get nearby planes, answered at once and not kept. |
| Why? | App functionality only. Not for ads, analytics, profiling or sale. |
| Do you share it? | The rounded position goes to adsb.lol (and our own relay if adsb.lol is busy) to get planes back. It is a service the app uses for the player. Apple: not linked to the player, not for tracking. Google: ephemeral, optional. The checklist says what to tick for a cautious reading. |
| Weather | A position rounded to about 10 km goes to a weather service (Open-Meteo or our own server). Not kept. |
| What else is collected? | A few anonymous usage notes (app opened, a catch made, errors), tied to a random ID made on the phone: no name, no location, no device ID, nothing that identifies a plane. The player can turn them off in Settings. |
| Accounts? | None. The collection stays on the phone. |
| Purchases? | Handled by the App Store or Google Play. We learn only that Plus is on. |
| Contacts, photos, camera, microphone? | Not used. |
| Ads, tracking, selling data? | None of them. |
| Encrypted in transit? | Yes (https). |
| Can a player delete their data? | There is no account. Settings → Reset everything clears the phone. |
| Privacy policy URL | https://skycollector.app/privacy (**must be rewritten for the stores first**, see 6.8). |
| Permission text the player sees | Use the drafts in the checklist ("Sky Collector uses your location so planes are drawn where they really are in your sky. Your exact position stays on your phone."). |

Apple vs Google differ on what "collected" means; the checklist spells out both. The one decision that is not copy is Google's "Shared": declare approximate location as shared if the reviewer reads adsb.lol as a third party (checklist, Google Play section).
