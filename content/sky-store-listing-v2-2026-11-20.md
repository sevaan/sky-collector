# Sky Collector: store listing v2 (merged), 2026-11-20

Written 2026-11-20 for Boss and Sevaan, who choose between this and what's saved in App Store Connect. **Nothing was pasted into a store.** One description, built from Sky Build's Version A (2,689 characters, in App Store Connect now) and my Version B (3,681), following the outside review: B's substance, A's tighter voice, the collecting-game feeling first, mechanics cut, no promise of two cards or a route on every catch.

Spelling is Canadian/British. No exclamation marks. Counts are real counts of the text as written. Limits: App Store description 4,000, promo 170, subtitle 30, keywords 100; Play short 80, full 4,000.

## Already decided by Sevaan (unchanged)

- Name: `Sky Collector`
- Subtitle: `Spot planes, collect cards` (26)
- Promotional text (166): Hold your phone up to the sky and collect the planes, helicopters and jets passing over you. Most airline flights get a card with the airline and route. Free, no ads.
- **Plane alerts are free** (decided 2026-11-20). The word "undecided" is gone from the listing.

## 1. App Store description (3,008 of 4,000; target about 3,000)

```
Look up. That plane has a name and a story. Sky Collector turns it into a card.

The sky becomes a collecting game. Point your phone at a plane, line it up in the circle and tap Collect. Every plane overhead is a chance to find something new, finish a set, earn a patch or catch something rare.

WHAT YOU CAN COLLECT
• Every kind of plane, from light aircraft to jumbo jets: who built it, when it first flew and one true fact
• Airline flights: most get a second card with the airline and, when we know it, the route
• Helicopters, gliders, hot-air balloons and airships, when one goes by
• Rare visitors, like giant freighters and old classics, take patience

SHINY CARDS
Some catches come out shiny: a plane beside the Moon, a plane in the golden light of sunset or sunrise, or a rare plane in colours you haven't collected before.

SEE WHAT'S COMING OVER
Today shows the best plane in your sky right now and what's due in the next 15 minutes. Tap Show me and the app points the way. No motion sensors? Drag the sky with a finger.

YOUR REGULARS
After a few days Sky Collector learns the airline flights that pass you at about the same time each day, so you know when to look up. It works this out on your phone.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• New missions every day and a new themed week every Monday
• Nineteen sets to fill, from The 737 Family and Giants to Short Hops and The Long Way
• More than 100 patches to earn
• Catch the same plane again on other days and its card levels up, from bronze to silver to gold
• Your day waits as a pack of cards to tear open the next morning

SEND CARDS TO A FRIEND
Send cards as a link and your friend opens them as a pack. Or send a sample pack that opens in any browser.

ALERTS (optional)
Turn on alerts and, when you leave the app, Sky Collector can set a heads-up for a rare plane or a new card due over you in the next few minutes, or for a regular flight you haven't caught. Three a day at most, never in your quiet hours. It can't spot new planes while the app is closed.

FREE. NO ADS. NO LOOT BOXES.
Every card, set, patch and mission is free. Rarity is never something you pay for.

GOOD TO KNOW
Planes come from volunteers with receivers, so coverage is strongest in North America and Europe and thinner elsewhere, and not every aircraft shows up. Private, business, military, police and government aircraft show only the kind of aircraft. No account. Your exact location stays on your phone.

SKY COLLECTOR PLUS (optional)
Every card back, seven more app icons, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and a members-only patch. Monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) or once, for good. Works with Family Sharing.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy: https://skycollector.app/privacy
```

## 5. What I checked, and what I could not

**Checked in Sky's code (2026-11-20)**
- 19 sets (`SKY_SETS.length`; names include The 737 Family, Giants, Short Hops, The Long Way).
- Weeks start Monday (`weekStartOf`).
- Alerts: off until turned on, apps only; set when you leave the app; 3 a day, 30 minutes apart, never in quiet hours (`js/sky-alerts.js`).
- Shiny moments (`js/shiny.js`), regulars (`js/sky-regulars.js`), morning pack, send cards.

**Could not check**
1. **Patch count.** "More than 100": Sky Dev's figure (130), not counted by me.
2. **Widgets.** Sky Build says the widget extension ships; Sky Dev says its code still carries Space ideas. "Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets" is a weaker claim than "for your regular flights" and is what I'd keep until Sky Dev confirms.
3. **Plus Family Sharing** is from `docs/STORES.md`, not seen in App Store Connect.
4. **Keywords.** Sky Build's saved list contains `tracker` and `radar`: they pull in flight-tracking searches and read as following planes, which Sky does not do. Mine (95) leaves them out:
   `aircraft,airplane,aviation,flight,helicopter,jet,airline,airport,spotter,avgeek,identify,flying`
   Sevaan chooses. Neither list has a competitor or airline name.
5. I did not run the app or see either draft on a device.
