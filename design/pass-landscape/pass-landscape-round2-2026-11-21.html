A shorter boarding pass

Round 2 after Sevaan's notes: two shorter proportions of L1 (photo on the left, stub on the right). The long side stays 42em, the plane card's height, so the pass grows taller instead of narrower: 2 : 1 is 42 × 21em and 1.7 : 1 is 42 × 24.7em. Behind the card it peeks out the top, centred; brought forward it turns −90° (you read it from the right side of the screen) with a slight tilt, at the plane card's own scale. Turning the phone just shows that same screen the right way up.
