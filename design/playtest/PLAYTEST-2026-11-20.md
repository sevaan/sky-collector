One pass over the web build (WebKit and Chromium through Playwright at 360x780, 390x844 and 430x932, day 13:30 and night
22:30 at Toronto Pearson with the recorded 57-aircraft feed, plus a 72-airborne copy of it for load), the iPhone 17
simulator (live feed, Toronto, 1:20 AM: a real quiet sky) and the Android emulator (see the end). Screenshots are in this

on the version line).

2026-11-19 move to Cloud Run quietly hit the live relay (2 planes instead of the recording's 24). It now fakes
`a.run.app/planes` too.

### 1. Find was empty all session (fixed)
Steps: fresh start, Explore with 24 planes up, open Collection → Find. It said "Keep Sky Collector open under the sky…"
however long the app had been open. Cause: `js/sky-local.js` kept its first read of `skyLocal` forever; the Collection's
frame reads it once at boot, before Explore has saved a tally, and never again. Fix: `localStats()` re-reads what another
page saved (at most every 3 s, never over this page's own unsaved tallies). After: "Flown over you, not caught yet" lists
the Dash 8-400, CRJ900, A350 and the rest with "Seen 2 times near you".
Screens: `before-find-empty.png`, `fixed-find-tab.png`.

### 2. Airport vehicles as cards (fixed in the feed)
The recorded feed has ESC155 type `SERV`, category C2 (a service vehicle). On the ground it is dropped, but anything in
categories C1-C7 (emergency and service vehicles, obstacles) that reports a numeric height went through and became an
"Aircraft · Everything Else · Rare" card (the seeded collection shows it in Albums). Fix: `js/planes.js` parse drops
C1-C7 and the pseudo-types SERV/EMER/GND/TWR/GRND. Already-caught SERV cards stay (not touched). Screen: `albums-night.png`
(seeded data), test in `tests/playtest-2026-11-20.test.mjs`.

### 3. Today at night, dark on dark (fixed)
Steps: night, Today, scroll to Today so far. "United · 4,341 km from Boston, over you at 10:30 PM", the 12 AM … 11 PM axis
and "Counts only the hours…" were #33495f on navy: the age-playtest readability rule had no night guard. Fix in
`css/sky-night.css`. Screens: `before-today-so-far-night.png`, `fixed-today-so-far-night.png`.

### 5. Sky frames with many planes (not fixed)
72 airborne planes, Chromium. 1x: pan p50 8.8 ms, p95 11.7, max 22, none over 33 ms. At 4x CPU (an older phone): idle p50
28 ms, pan p50 36 ms with 155 of 261 frames over 33 ms; the sky frame (`main.js:8623`) costs about 6.6 ms of script a frame
at 4x while panning. WebKit (no throttle): 17 ms frames throughout, max 30. So a busy-airport sky on an older Android will
stutter when you swing the phone. Worth a profile of the plane draw path (labels and trails per plane) before release.

## Performance numbers
- Cold start to HUD: 1,018 ms (Chromium), 1,049 ms (WebKit); with 100 catches 1,036 / 1,043 ms.
- Card viewer swipe (swipe-frames.mjs): Chromium 4x 0 frames over 20 ms, longest 19 ms, finger-to-move 36 ms median;
  WebKit 3 over 20 ms, longest 24 ms, 15 ms.
- Pack opening (night pack): Chromium max 23 ms; WebKit one 82 ms frame at the start.
- Idle: Today 20 s: 44 ms script, 795 style recalcs; Collection 20 s: 22 ms script but 2,422 style recalcs (121/s,
  CSS animation in the frame: cheap, but it keeps the GPU busy; worth checking on battery). The toast stack check runs
  every 300 ms in both pages (0.01 ms each).
- Memory: 15 minutes in Explore with 72 planes and Today/Collection visits every 5 min: heap 38.3 → 42.3 MB (flat from minute 11), DOM nodes
  flat after the first Collection visit, listeners 642 → 657. No leak seen.
- Plane feed: website, the Cloud Run relay every 10 s whichever tab is open (6 a minute, 360 an hour). Phone (plane-feed.js
  defaults, no dial): idle-only 120 adsb.lol requests an hour; aiming 30% of the time 192 an hour; aiming the whole time
  360 an hour. Three hours of keen use spends the 500; lowAt/switchAt then slow and hand over to the relay.

## Experience, as two players
- Plane spotter: Likes the real types, registrations, operators and routes, the radar labels and "Seen 2 times near you"
  on Find. Will notice: "Everything Else" for a 757-200; Rare "Aircraft" cards for junk types; constellations on the
  ground; a whole day's missions done by lunch.
- Casual player: The first catch is quick and the reveal is a treat. After that, with nothing in view there's no nudge
  where to turn, and once three missions are ticked there's no reason to come back today. A quiet sky at 1 AM still paid
  off: Today's "Up now" led straight to an Epic 747-8.
- Pace (seeded, about 12 days): 7 catches → Plane Spotter 558 XP, 5 patches; 21 → 1,464 XP, 13 patches, 3 bronze albums;
  70 → Tracker 3,017 XP, 31 patches, Long Haul gold. Patches come fast early, ranks slow.

## Options for Sevaan
1. Missions: (a) a fourth "bonus" mission that unlocks after the three; (b) size by catches so far today (the first
   needs 1 catch, the last needs 5); (c) keep three but make one of them a stretch (a Giant, a new airline).
2. Time-aware missions: skip helicopter/low-plane goals at night, or pick from what the feed shows right now.
3. Empty view: an edge arrow to the nearest uncaught plane after 3 s of nothing in view, or a "nearest: SE, 8° up" line
   under the radar.
4. Ground chart: drop constellation lines/names below the horizon in Sky (keep stars), or keep them only at night.
5. Unknown types: make unlisted types Uncommon instead of Rare unless the feed has a description; show the type code
   ("ZZZZ") instead of "Aircraft".
6. Albums: show plane albums as "N collected" toward gold (like flight albums) instead of "0 / 1".
7. Patch counter: count only patches you can see (or always include all secrets as "?") so the total doesn't move.
8. Older phones: thin the per-plane drawing when more than ~40 are up (labels only near the circle).
9. Testing tools: a Sky set for Test phone notifications (Plane alert, Regular, Today), the Space ones hidden.
