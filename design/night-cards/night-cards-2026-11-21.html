Sky Collector · design pass · 2026-11-21

Night rank card and route map

At night the Collection is navy, but the rank card and the route map are still cream paper with a pale sky, so they glare. Three night looks, each on a 375px Collection with the real two-card deck (one card in front, the other's strip peeking above at .94). The pictures are the app's own builders, climbSvg (js/rank-climb.js) and routeMap (js/route-map.js), restyled with CSS only. Stars are generated once (seeded) and baked into the CSS; a few twinkle, and they hold still with Reduce Motion.

Sample: Spotter, 680 XP, 1,320 to Type Spotter. Twelve passes over Toronto to Delhi, Seoul, Dubai, Milan, London and others.

NOWToday's night cards

For comparison: the cream paper dimmed and warmed. It still reads as the brightest thing on the screen.

ANight paper

The same printed card, on deep navy stock with a faint grain and cream ink. The climb is a night sky with a moon, the Milky Way and twinkling stars; the plane and its trail glow warm red, the runway has lights. The map is a dark ocean with slightly lighter land, amber arcs glowing like flight paths at night, airports lit like cities.

BGlass cockpit

Near-black glass like a cockpit display: cyan readouts and level lines, amber for you (your plane, your XP, your longest route). Faint scan lines and sparse stars in the climb; the map becomes a navigation display with range rings, outline coasts and airports as cyan rings.

CClimb into the night

The climb tells the time of day: a last glow of sunset and city lights at the runway, deep blue higher up, and more stars the higher the level, so climbing the ranks is climbing into the stars. The map matches: Earth at night, airports as glowing cities, moonlit pale arcs, the longest route in the same warm coral as your plane, a hint of dusk along the bottom.
