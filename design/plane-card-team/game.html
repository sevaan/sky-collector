Plane card: game-first

Rarity and level first, the way a collection game card reads: a coloured rarity badge, a level medal with a ring that fills toward the next level, and the plain words "2 of 10 different A350s for Silver". A bigger photo, the model name huge, the specs as four icon stats, your catch squeezed into two lines. The fact, the airline's registration and the long numbers go to the back. Same card: the A350-900, Common, Bronze, caught Nov 21 from a Lufthansa flight.

Now

Today's card

The app's own planeCardHtml. Twelve blocks, most the same size and weight, so nothing reads first. Rarity is a small grey pill, the level a chip at the bottom, and "2 AIRCRAFT · 2 LIVERIES" doesn't say what's being counted.

A

Medal & Ring

Rarity badge and set across the top, a big photo framed in the rarity colour, the level medal sitting on the photo's corner with its ring two-tenths full. The model name huge under the maker. A white "level" box spells it out with ten pips. Four icon stats, then your catch in two lines.

Collection tiles, one per rarity (the rarity strip carries the colour)
B

Nameplate & Road

The photo runs edge to edge, with the rarity on a ribbon. A cream nameplate overlaps it, holding the name and the level medal. Under it, the road to the next level: your bronze medal, ten pips, then the silver medal greyed out until you earn it, so you can see what's next. Stats in four tiles. Your catch sits on a navy ticket stub at the bottom.

Collection tiles through the levels: not caught yet, Bronze, Silver, Gold

My pick: B, with A's level box wording

B's road (your medal, the pips, the next medal greyed out) is the strongest "I want the next one" moment. It shows the reward instead of naming it. The edge-to-edge photo with the rarity ribbon and the nameplate looks most like a pro card. Take A's one-line rule ("Same plane twice doesn't count. Find new ones!") into B, because that line is what clears up "2 AIRCRAFT".

  • For the reveal: the medal ring filling one pip, and the pip glowing (both variants already animate the newest pip).
  • Watch out: long names (Boeing KC-135R Stratotanker) need the model line to shrink, as today's fc-name--long does.
  • The back takes the fact, every registration you've caught, the airlines seen ("liveries"), the heading compass, the country and the year.
